Voice & setup

This uses your browser/OS's built-in voices, which is why it's free. Quality varies a lot by browser — Chrome on a Mac or Edge on Windows tend to have the most natural "Natural"/"Online" voices. When you're ready for something closer to a real human voice, the next step is wiring in ElevenLabs or Cartesia's API instead of this dropdown — same interface, better voice.

When this is on, the camera stays active and Cartel looks at the current frame each time you speak to it — that's how it "familiarizes" itself over time. It does not stream continuously to save your free-tier usage, and you'll always see a red dot + live thumbnail while it's on. Turn it off here any time.

This is on by default, since you said you're fine with it. It means your browser's speech recognizer is always running in the background (the teal "listening for Cartel" dot confirms it) — your mic audio is continuously being streamed to your browser's speech service, not just while mid-command. Say "Cartel" or "hey Cartel" followed by what you want. Turn it off here any time if you change your mind.

Say "call <name>" — the first time for someone new, Cartel will ask you for their number once and remember it (saved only in this browser), then use your phone's own dialer. No calls are placed by Cartel itself.

Optional. Leave blank to keep using your browser's free voice. If you add a free ElevenLabs account, grab an API key and a voice ID from their site and paste them here — Cartel will use that instead, no code changes needed. Stored only in this browser, never sent anywhere but ElevenLabs.
